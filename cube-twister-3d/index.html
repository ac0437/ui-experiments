Press Scramble to start. Drag a sticker to turn a layer, drag the background to look around.

How to play

Turn a layer: press on a sticker and drag in the direction you want it to move.

Rotate view: drag anywhere off the cube.

Keyboard: U D L R F B turn faces clockwise, hold Shift for counter‑clockwise. M E S turn middle slices. Ctrl/⌘+Z undo. H hint.

Hints: 💡 works out a short solution from wherever your cube is and shows the next move, flashing the layer to turn. “R” means turn that face clockwise as if you were looking straight at it; R' is counter‑clockwise; R2 is a half turn.

Colors: white top (U), green front (F), red right (R).

The timer starts on your first move after a scramble. Using Auto‑solve doesn’t count toward your best time.
